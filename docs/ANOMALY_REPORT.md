# Anomaly detection: how well does it catch errors?

_Generated by `python -m pipeline.anomaly_eval`._

The history is rebuilt from news stories, so the errors that matter are typing errors in a story. Four checks look for them (see `pipeline/anomaly.py`): a robust z-score against a trend line through the 7 days either side, the same test after removing the yearly cycle with STL, an Isolation Forest over each day's changes, and a water balance (the lake level must move the way inflow minus outflow says). A value is queued for review when the evidence adds up (two strong signals, or one strong and two moderate), or the local check alone is extreme.

## On the real history

126 of 4,549 dam-days have a queued value (28 per 1,000). Most are clear typos (outflows of 20 or 35 cusecs where 35,000 is usual, a Mangla inflow ten times its neighbours); some are real sudden changes, which is why a person decides.

## 20 planted errors, 10 random draws

Each draw plants 20 errors at random clean days from 2017 on, at least 20 days apart on the same dam. **Caught 62% on average** (range 45% to 80% across draws), with 3.3 extra queued values per draw caused by the planted ones (neighbours that look odd next to an error).

| Kind of error | Planted | Caught | Typical size of the change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| one digit wrong (28,800 → 38,800) | 40 | 9 (22%) | median 15% |
| two digits swapped (28,800 → 82,800) | 30 | 12 (40%) | median 22% |
| a digit dropped (145,000 → 14,500) | 20 | 19 (95%) | median 900% |
| a digit doubled (28,800 → 288,800) | 10 | 10 (100%) | median 900% |
| inflow and outflow swapped | 30 | 24 (80%) | median 87% |
| the other dam's inflow | 30 | 20 (67%) | median 188% |
| level typo (1,510.82 → 1,501.82) | 40 | 31 (78%) | median 10.0 ft |

**Errors big enough to matter** (a flow off by 15% or more, a level off by 1 ft or more): caught 125 of 164 (76%). Smaller ones: 0 of 36. A slip in the third digit (28,800 → 28,300) is within normal day-to-day change and can't be told apart from a real reading.

## Days where two outlets disagreed

For each of the days where outlets reported different inflows, the other outlet's figure was put in place of the one used:

| Disagreement | Days | Other figure queued |
| --- | --- | --- |
| under 5% | 23 | 1 |
| 5% to 20% | 35 | 0 |
| over 20% | 15 | 4 |

Small disagreements are usually two reporting times or rounding, and neither figure is wrong enough to stand out. Disagreements over 5% go to the review queue regardless.

## Limits

- Errors that repeat for several days (a story copied forward with the same typo) look like a real change; the checks compare a value with its neighbours.
- Thin stretches of history (2017 to 2019) have fewer neighbours, so fewer values can be checked.
- Outflows change in steps by operator decision, so outflow checks are looser than inflow checks.
